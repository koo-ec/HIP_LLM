Paper and replication implementation
====================================

The lower-level ``hip_llm`` package contains the tested replication of the
hierarchical Bayesian and imprecise-probability reliability method described in:

R. Aghazadeh-Chakherlou, Q. Guo, S. Khastgir, P. Popov, X. Zhang and X. Zhao,
*A hierarchical imprecise probability approach to reliability assessment of
large language models*, Reliability Engineering & System Safety 272 (2026),
112615. DOI: `10.1016/j.ress.2026.112615 <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ress.2026.112615>`_.

See the repository's `paper overview <https://github.com/koo-ec/HIP_LLM/blob/main/docs/paper-overview.md>`_
and `replication report <https://github.com/koo-ec/HIP_LLM/blob/main/results/reproduction_report.md>`_
for the method, provenance and reproduced results.
